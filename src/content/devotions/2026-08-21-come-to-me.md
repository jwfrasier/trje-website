"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."

Notice who Jesus invites. Not the rested. Not the people who have it figured out. The weary. The burdened. The ones carrying more than they were built to carry.

And notice what he offers. Not a system. Not a checklist. Himself. *Come to me.*

Most of us have tried to find rest in the things around Jesus: in being at church, in doing the right things, in finally getting our lives in order. Those things are not bad. But they are not him. And he is the only one who can actually give the rest he is talking about.

So today, whatever you are carrying, take him at his word. Come to him. Not to a better you. To him.

**Pray:** Jesus, I am tired. I come to you, not to a plan or a performance. Teach me what it means to rest in you today.
