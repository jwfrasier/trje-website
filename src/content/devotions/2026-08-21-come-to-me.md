Hey brothers and sisters. I’ve been praying Gen. 1:1 over you this AM. 

As we journey through this first book of The Bible, we will be immersed in seeing the loving presence of God, not only towards humans, but also with His creation. 

We are told it is God who created the heavens (the cosmos) and the earth. This is critical in our understanding of how we walk with Jesus. Afterall, we are told in Col. 1 it was actually Jesus who created. One way to see it is for Jesus (The Son) to start creating with God (Dad) right next to Him. As He creates, imagine God gazing at Him with eternal excitement saying “Wow!! This is amazing and so are You! Let’s make some more!” 

Next, I used to believe and say that God created Creation out of nothing (in Latin it’s called Ex Nihilo). I no longer believe this. It is more that God created out of the overflow of love and His creative power. This is definitely not nothing! It also does not mean that somehow Creation is God in any form. 

Therefore, today, I pray you take time to see the loving interaction between Dad and Son. I also pray you see it is not as much that He created something out of nothing. It is that Creation is an outworking of His creative love. As we see this, it by no means we worship Creation, but it definitely can and should lead us to worship the God of Creation, while also seeing we are continually surrounded by the loving sacredness of His good Creation.
